# Taxi Tycoon Privacy Policy (Huawei AppGallery)

_Last updated: 12 November 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game **Taxi Tycoon** (the "Game") collects in its **Huawei AppGallery** version, why, and what choices you have. The Game does not require an account. Coins, experience, boosters and VIP can be bought through Huawei In-App Purchases. The AppGallery version **shows no ads** and does not use your advertising ID.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is **Aliaksei Kireishyn**, an individual developer, Minsk, Republic of Belarus.
Contact for any privacy question or request: **taxitycoon.support@gmail.com**

## 2. Game data on your device and on our server

The nickname, company name, logo, start country and all game progress are stored in the Game's save file on your device. After your first finished ride a copy of this file is also stored on our server, so you can get your progress back after reinstalling the Game or on another phone with your recovery code. Uninstalling the Game deletes the file on your device, not the copy on the server: see [Delete your data](https://kireishin.github.io/taxitycoon.github.io/delete-data/).

## 3. Data we collect and why

The Game uses Huawei's **AppGallery Connect** and **HMS** services (Aspiegel / Huawei Technologies) and Google's **Firebase** platform (provided in the EEA, UK and Switzerland by Google Ireland Limited, and elsewhere by Google LLC). They act as our service providers. On Huawei devices without Google services, Firebase does not run and collects nothing.

| Service | What is collected | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Huawei In-App Purchases** (HMS IAP Kit) | Purchases are handled by Huawei (sign-in with your HUAWEI ID). We receive the product, price, currency, order id, a purchase token and Huawei's signature of the purchase, never your payment details or HUAWEI ID | To deliver what you bought and check that the purchase is genuine | **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| **AppGallery Connect Crash** | Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, a random installation identifier, the device language and the number of app launches | To find and fix crashes and errors | Our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Game working (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Firebase Analytics** (only on devices with Google services) | A random app-instance identifier; gameplay events (for example tutorial completed, city unlocked, ride finished, level reached, coin balance, the start country you picked in the Game); in-app purchase events (product, price, currency, transaction id); device model, operating system, app version, language; approximate location (country / region) that Google derives from the IP address | To understand how the Game is played and improve its balance, content and stability | **Your consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in improving the Game (Art. 6(1)(f)); you can switch it off at any time |
| **Firebase Crashlytics** (only on devices with Google services) | Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, free memory / disk, app version, a random Crashlytics installation identifier, the device language and the number of app launches | To find and fix crashes and errors | Our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Game working (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Firebase Remote Config** (only on devices with Google services) | A random Firebase installation identifier and app version | To deliver game balance settings without an app update | Our **legitimate interest** in operating the Game (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| **Cloud saves** (our server on Cloudflare) | A copy of your save file (nickname, company name, logo, game progress, settings), a player ID, a recovery code, a hash of the device identifier, app version, store, the IP address while a request is handled | To bring your progress back after a reinstall or on another phone, and to fix problems at your request | **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) |

We do **not** collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID, and we do **not** sell your data.

## 4. Your choice about analytics

- **EEA, UK and Switzerland:** on first launch the Game asks whether you allow analytics. Until you answer, and if you decline, Firebase Analytics collects nothing.
- **Everyone:** you can turn analytics on or off at any time in **Settings → Privacy**. Turning it off stops analytics collection and deletes the analytics data and identifier stored on your device.

Crash reports (AppGallery Connect Crash, and Firebase Crashlytics where it runs) are always sent, because the Game cannot be kept working without them.

## 5. How long data is kept

- Analytics data: 2 months, then deleted automatically by Google.
- Crash reports: 90 days.
- Purchase records needed for accounting: as long as the law requires.
- Cloud saves: until you ask us to delete them. If the server storage is nearly full, we delete players who have not played for over 12 months.

## 6. International transfers

Huawei processes AppGallery Connect and HMS data according to its own privacy terms: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Transfers from the EEA, UK and Switzerland rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://policies.google.com/privacy

Cloud saves are handled by Cloudflare, Inc., which may process data in the United States and other countries: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect earlier processing). Write to **taxitycoon.support@gmail.com**. We may ask for your player ID (in Settings) or for details that let us find your data. We answer within one month. To delete your account and cloud saves, use **Settings → Data → Request data deletion** or follow [these steps](https://kireishin.github.io/taxitycoon.github.io/delete-data/).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live.

## 8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has used the Game and analytics data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

Data sent to Huawei, Firebase and our server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your device is stored in the Game's private storage.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy, for example when the Game adds new features. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will also be announced in the Game.
