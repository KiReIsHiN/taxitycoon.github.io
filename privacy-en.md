# Taxi Tycoon Privacy Policy (RuStore)

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game **Taxi Tycoon** (the "Game") collects in its **RuStore** version, why, and what choices you have. The Game does not require an account. This version has no purchases for money. The Game shows rewarded ads, but only when you tap the watch button yourself.

The Russian version of this Policy is the main one; this is a translation.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The personal data operator is **Aliaksei Kireishyn**, an individual, the developer of the Game, Minsk, Republic of Belarus.
Contact for any question or request about personal data: **taxitycoon.support@gmail.com**

## 2. Data that stays on your device

The nickname, company name, logo, start country and all game progress are stored **only on your device**, in the Game's save file. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the Game deletes them.

## 3. Data we collect and why

The Game uses services of third-party companies. They process data on our behalf or under their own terms (links in section 6).

| Service | What is collected | Why | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Firebase Analytics** (Google) | A random app-instance identifier; gameplay events (for example tutorial completed, city unlocked, ride finished, level reached, coin balance, rewarded ad watched); device model, OS and app version, language; approximate location (country / region) that Google derives from the IP address (Google does not store the IP address itself) | To understand how the Game is played and improve its balance, content and stability | **Your consent** |
| **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) | Crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS version, free memory / disk, app version, a random installation identifier, the device language, the number of app launches | To find and fix crashes | Our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Game working |
| **Firebase Remote Config** (Google) | A random Firebase installation identifier, app version | To change the game balance without an app update | Our **legitimate interest** in operating the Game |
| **Yandex Advertising Network** (Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, Yandex LLC) | The device advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device model, OS version, language, mobile carrier, ad impressions and clicks, approximate location from the IP address | To show rewarded ads (they give you coins, experience or a booster) and to fund the development of the Game | Showing an ad — at your request (you tap "Watch ad"). **Personalisation** of ads by your interests — only with **your consent**; without it ads are not personalised |
| **RuStore SDK** (VK LLC) | Asking for a rating and checking for updates through the RuStore app: app id, version, your RuStore account details (processed by RuStore, not passed to us) | So that you can rate and update the Game without leaving it | Our **legitimate interest**; works only when RuStore is installed |
| **Tracer** (RuStore's error analytics, VK LLC) | Crash, freeze (ANR) and error reports: stack traces, device model, OS and app version, a random installation identifier, the device language, the number of app launches | To find and fix crashes | Our **legitimate interest** in keeping the Game working |

We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do **not** sell your data.

## 4. Your choice: analytics and personalised ads

- On first launch the Game asks whether you allow **analytics and personalised ads**. Until you answer, and if you decline, Firebase Analytics collects nothing and Yandex ads are shown without personalisation.
- You can change your choice at any time: **Settings → Privacy → Analytics and personalised ads**. Turning it off stops analytics collection and deletes the analytics data and identifier stored on your device.
- You can also reset the advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in the Android settings: "Settings → Google → Ads".
- You only watch ads yourself: they never appear without a tap on the watch button.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics and Tracer) are always sent, because the Game cannot be kept working without them.

## 5. How long data is kept

- Analytics data: 2 months, then deleted automatically by Google.
- Crash reports: 90 days (Firebase Crashlytics), 90 days (Tracer).
- Ad network data: under the terms of Yandex LLC (section 6).

## 6. Data transfers and third-party terms

Google may process data in the United States and other countries: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy, https://policies.google.com/privacy
Yandex Advertising Network: https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/ and https://yandex.ru/legal/mobileads_sdk_agreement/
RuStore and Tracer (VK LLC): https://www.rustore.ru/help/legal/ and https://apptracer.ru/

Data of users from Russia is processed under Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data". Data of users from Belarus is processed under Law of the Republic of Belarus No. 99-Z "On Personal Data Protection".

## 7. Your rights

You can ask for information about the processing of your data, ask to correct, block or delete it, and withdraw your consent at any time (withdrawing does not affect earlier processing). Write to **taxitycoon.support@gmail.com**. Because the Game has no accounts, we may ask you for details that let us find your data (for example the approximate date you played and your device model). We answer within 30 days.

You also have the right to contact the authority that protects the rights of personal data subjects (in Russia — Roskomnadzor, in Belarus — the National Personal Data Protection Center).

## 8. Children

The Game is intended for people aged 12 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data of younger children. If you believe a child has used the Game and data about them was collected, write to us and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

Data is sent to the services encrypted (HTTPS). Data on your device is stored in the Game's private storage.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy, for example when new features are added. The date at the top shows the current version; the Game will tell you about important changes.
